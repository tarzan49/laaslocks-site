Battery &
charging.

USB-C power, the charging light, and what to do when the battery runs low.

USB-C CHARGING

Leave it on
the bike.

Read the steps ↓
  1. 01

    Lift the cover.

    Lift the rubber cover over the USB-C port.

    Enlarge ↗
  2. 02

    Plug it in.

    Connect a USB-C charger, laptop or power bank. A phone works too if it supports USB-C power output.

    Enlarge ↗
  3. 03

    Wait for the light to go out.

    The LED lights up while charging and switches off when the battery is full. Then unplug the cable and close the rubber cover.

    Enlarge ↗
Wait for the LED to switch off. The battery percentage in the app may show full before charging is finished.

A charge lasts roughly six months with normal use. Check its battery level in LAAS; the app also warns you when power is low.

LOW BATTERY

Still get it open.

The app warns you before the battery runs low. If you need power now, connect a USB-C power bank. You can unlock while charging.

Do not charge the lock below 2°C. Let it warm up first.
