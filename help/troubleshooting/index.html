Try this.

  1. Press the button. No light? Plug it in and charge it. The battery might be empty.
    Show me
  2. Force close the LAAS app. Reopen it and try unlocking again.
    Show me

    iPhone

    Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause. Find LAAS, then swipe its preview up and off the screen. Open LAAS again and try unlocking.

    Have a Home button? Double-click it to see your open apps.

    Apple’s instructions ↗

    Android

    Open Settings → Apps → See all apps → LAAS. Tap Force stop and confirm. Open LAAS again and try unlocking.

    Menu names can vary between Android phones.

  3. Check that nothing is blocking the lock, such as a wheel spoke.
    Show me
  4. Call LAAS Support: +45 31 33 82 13

    Can’t reach us? Keep it moving.

    We cover a shared bike, public transport or another reasonable option, up to €30.

    If we have local service, we’ll take care of removing the old lock and installing a new one.

    If we don’t, we’ll cover up to €30 for removal and installation of the new lock we will send you.

    For two years from purchase.

    What isn’t covered?

    Low battery, phone problems, or anything else unrelated to a failure of your LAAS lock.

    How do I claim my expenses?

    Contact Support and send us your receipts.
