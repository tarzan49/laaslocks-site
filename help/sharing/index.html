Lend your bike.

Invitations, access levels, and how to remove access.

INVITE SOMEONE

Send a link.

Read the steps ↓
  1. 01

    Open sharing.

    Open your lock’s sharing controls in LAAS.

    Enlarge ↗
  2. 02

    Choose access.

    Choose Guest or Owner and add a name or note so you know who the invitation is for.

    Enlarge ↗
  3. 03

    Send the link.

    Tap Share and send the invitation in a message.

    Enlarge ↗
  4. 04

    They accept.

    They open the link and accept it in LAAS. No account is needed.

    Enlarge ↗
An invitation can be accepted once. It is not a one-use key: the person can keep unlocking until their access is removed.
CHOOSE ACCESS

Owner or Guest.

Guest
Can unlock the bike.
Owner
Can unlock and manage access. All owners are equal, including the ability to remove other owners.
MANAGE ACCESS

Take it back.

If the link has not been accepted, revoke the invitation. If the person has joined, open the access list, select their name and tap the trash icon.

Removing access does not require getting a physical key back.
