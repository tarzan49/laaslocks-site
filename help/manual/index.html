02 / RING-LOCK

My bike has mounting holes.

  1. 01

    Line it up.

    Hold the lock against the mounting holes. Choose the bracket slots that leave room above the tyre.

    Enlarge ↗
  2. 02

    Put in the screws.

    Put the supplied screws through the brackets and into the frame.

    Enlarge ↗
  3. 03

    Tighten and check.

    Centre the lock and tighten the screws with a 4 mm hex key. Check that the lock clears the tire.

    Enlarge ↗
Before you ride, make sure the lock clears the tire.

03 / RING-LOCK

My bike has no mounting holes.

  1. 01

    Attach the brackets.

    Put the metal nuts in the bracket slots. Screw the brackets loosely onto the lock. Leave room to adjust.

    Enlarge ↗
  2. 02

    Bend the strips.

    Gently bend the plastic strips around the frame.

    Enlarge ↗
  3. 03

    Wrap around the frame.

    Wrap the strips around the frame. Fit the plastic screws and tighten them lightly with a flathead screwdriver.

    Enlarge ↗
  4. 04

    Align and tighten.

    Centre the lock and leave room above the tyre. Tighten the screws that hold the lock, then the screws on the strips.

    Enlarge ↗
  5. 05

    Trim the strips.

    Trim the excess with pliers or a knife. Check that the lock is secure.

    Enlarge ↗
Before you ride, make sure the lock clears the tire.

06 / RING-LOCK

Battery and charging

  1. 01

    Lift the cover.

    Lift the rubber cover over the USB-C port.

    Enlarge ↗
  2. 02

    Plug it in.

    Connect a USB-C charger, laptop or power bank. A phone works too if it supports USB-C power output.

    Enlarge ↗
  3. 03

    Wait for the light to go out.

    The LED lights up while charging and switches off when the battery is full. Then unplug the cable and close the rubber cover.

    Enlarge ↗
Do not charge below 2°C. Wait for the LED to switch off; the app battery display can be inaccurate while charging.

07 / RING-LOCK

Apple Find My

  1. 01

    Press three times.

    Press the button on the side of your lock three times.

    Enlarge ↗
  2. 02

    Look for the blinking light.

    The LED blinks. Pairing mode stays active for ten minutes.

  3. 03

    Open Apple Find My.

    On your iPhone or iPad, open Find My. Go to Items, tap +, then Add Other Item.

    Enlarge ↗
  4. 04

    Connect your lock.

    Select Ring-Lock when it appears, tap Connect, then follow the steps to name it.

    Enlarge ↗
Your Ring-Lock is now in Apple Find My. Open Items and select it to see your bike on the map.

08 / RING-LOCK

My lock won’t open.

  1. Press the button. No light? Plug it in and charge it. The battery might be empty.
    Show me
  2. Force close the LAAS app. Reopen it and try unlocking again.
  3. Check that nothing is blocking the lock, such as a wheel spoke.
    Show me
  4. Call LAAS Support: +45 31 33 82 13
Open urgent help →

Get access back.

Signed in with Apple or Google? Sign in on another phone.

Another Owner has access? Have them invite your new phone or a phone you borrow.

If you're the only Owner and weren't signed in, contact LAAS Support. We'll verify you using information only you would know. There's then a six-hour security delay before access is reset.

No signal? No problem.

The last phone to connect to the lock while online can keep unlocking without internet. Unlock, ride into the woods, and use that same phone to get home.

A phone that has not connected to the lock yet needs internet first.
