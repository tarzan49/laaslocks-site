Mount your lock

My bike has no mounting holes.

You need Flex-Mount, a 4 mm hex key, a flathead screwdriver, and pliers or a knife.

Read the steps ↓
  1. 01

    Attach the brackets.

    Put the metal nuts in the bracket slots. Screw the brackets loosely onto the lock. Leave room to adjust.

    Enlarge ↗
  2. 02

    Bend the strips.

    Gently bend the plastic strips around the frame.

    Enlarge ↗
  3. 03

    Wrap around the frame.

    Wrap the strips around the frame. Fit the plastic screws and tighten them lightly with a flathead screwdriver.

    Enlarge ↗
  4. 04

    Align and tighten.

    Centre the lock and leave room above the tyre. Tighten the screws that hold the lock, then the screws on the strips.

    Enlarge ↗
  5. 05

    Trim the strips.

    Trim the excess with pliers or a knife. Check that the lock is secure.

    Enlarge ↗
Before you ride, make sure the lock clears the tire.
