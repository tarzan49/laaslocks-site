Do I need an account?+

No. Signing in with Google or Apple makes it easier to regain access if you lose your phone.

Do I need to pair it in Bluetooth settings?+

No. Add your lock in the LAAS app. It will never appear in your phone’s Bluetooth settings.

What comes in the box?+

LAAS Ring-Lock, mounting screws, manual on the back of the box, insurance certificate and return shipping label.

What if my bike has no mounting holes?+

Use Flex-Mount. It is an optional accessory for frames without ring-lock mounting holes.

Which bikes does it fit?+

Most city, commuter, hybrid, electric, cargo and family bikes. It is not designed for fat bikes. Try it for 57 days; if it does not fit, send it back.

What if it doesn’t fit my bike?+

Send it back using the return shipping label in the box.

Do I lock it with the app?+

No. Lock it by hand. Use the LAAS app to unlock it over Bluetooth.

Can I unlock from far away?+

Unlocking uses Bluetooth, so you need to be within approximately 10 metres of your bike.

What if the shackle is obstructed?+

Check that nothing is obstructing the shackle, such as a wheel spoke.

How do I lend someone my bike?+

Choose Owner or Guest access in the LAAS app and send an invitation link. The other person opens the link to accept.

Can an invitation link be used twice?+

No. Each invitation can be accepted once. That does not limit the person to one unlock; their access continues until removed.

Can I cancel an invitation?+

Yes. Revoke an unused invitation link in the app. If it has already been accepted, remove that person’s access instead.

What is the difference between Owner and Guest?+

A Guest can unlock. An Owner can also manage the lock and access, including removing other owners. All owners are equal.

Does a guest need an account?+

No. They can accept access without creating an account.

How often does it need charging?+

Roughly every six months.

How do I charge it?+

Lift the rubber cover and plug a USB-C cable into the lock. It can stay on the bike while charging.

Can I unlock while charging?+

Yes. Connect a USB-C power bank and unlock through the app.

Can I charge it from my phone?+

Yes, if your phone can supply power through USB-C. That includes USB-C Android phones with power output and iPhone 15 or newer.

Can I charge my phone from the lock?+

No. LAAS is not a festival power bank.

Will the app warn me about a low battery?+

Yes. The app provides low-battery notifications.

What else does the app show?+

Your locks, their battery levels, shared access and available firmware updates.

How do I update the lock?+

When a firmware update is available, the app notifies you and guides you through it.

How safe is Ring-Lock?+

Ring-Lock has passed Denmark’s strict Varefakta insurance tests. It is insurance approved in Denmark.

Does it work on Android?+

Yes. Use LAAS on Android to unlock and share your bike. The Android app is available on Google Play. Apple Find My requires an Apple device.

What if my phone is lost or replaced?+

  1. Signed in with Apple or Google?

    Install the LAAS app on another phone. Sign in with the same account.

  2. Another Owner has access?

    Have them invite your new phone — or a phone you borrow.

  3. Neither of those works? Contact Support.

Do I find and unlock the bike in the same app?+

No. Locate the bike in Apple Find My. Unlock it in the LAAS app.

Is Find My live GPS?+

No. Nearby participating Apple devices help update the location. It is not a continuous live GPS feed.

Can LAAS see where my bike is?+

No. Your bike’s location is handled through Apple Find My with end-to-end encryption. Neither LAAS nor Apple can see it.

What if my bike is missing?+

  1. Check Apple Find My.

    Open the Apple Find My app, tap Items and select your Ring-Lock. Check the location on the map and the time it was last seen.

    On Android? Use the iPhone or iPad linked to your lock in Find My.

  2. It might have been moved.

    Look around the place you parked: the next bike rack, the other side of the station, or a nearby parking area. It’s worth checking even if Find My has no new location.

  3. No new location?

    In your Ring-Lock’s details in Find My, turn on Notify When Found. You’ll get a notification when it’s located again.

  4. Still missing?

    Mark it as lost in Find My and add a phone number or email where someone who finds it can reach you. Depending on your version, look for Lost Mode or Show Contact Info.

What tools do I need for frame mounting?+

The supplied M5 screws and a 4 mm hex key. The hex key is not included.

What tools do I need for Flex-Mount?+

A 4 mm hex key, a flathead screwdriver and pliers or a knife to trim the clamp strips.

How do I know charging is finished?+

The LED on the lock switches off. The app battery percentage may be inaccurate during charging.

Can I charge the lock in freezing weather?+

Do not charge below 2°C. Let the lock warm up first.

How do I put it in Find My pairing mode?+

Press the button on the side of the lock three times. The LED blinks and pairing mode stays active for ten minutes.
